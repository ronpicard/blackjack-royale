# Changelog

All notable changes to Blackjack Royale are documented in this file, following the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) format.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-25

### Added

- A 3D blackjack table in a casino: a felt half-moon table with a padded rail, a card shoe and discard tray, a chip tray along the dealer's edge, and a dim casino floor of slot machines, chandeliers, gaming tables and marquee lights.
- Six-deck blackjack, dealt one card at a time from the shoe: dealer stands on soft 17, blackjack pays 3 to 2, double on any first two cards including after a split, split any equal-rank pair up to four hands, split aces get one card each, and insurance pays 2 to 1 when the dealer peeks a blackjack.
- A `HINT` toggle that marks the basic-strategy play with a gold glow.
- Chips of 1, 5, 25, 100 and 500 credits, with undo, clear, rebet and double the bet.
- A camera that follows the game from seated at the table to in close over the cards, plus seated, over-the-cards and overhead views.
- Quick deal, which deals and flips at double speed without changing the cards.
- Spectators who cheer your wins, louder for blackjack or a big win, under a shower of confetti, and groan at your losses.
- Credits, history and settings saved in your browser, with a refill when you run out. Play credits only: no real money and no purchases.
- Keyboard controls, and touch controls sized for phones in both orientations.
- An attract mode that plays the table by itself with basic strategy behind the menu.
- A headless simulator (`npm run simulate`) that plays rounds with basic strategy and reports the realised return.
- Synthesised casino sound effects with a mute toggle.
- Deployed to GitHub Pages on every push to `main`.
